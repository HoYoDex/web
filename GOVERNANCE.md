# Governance

HoYoDex is small. This document describes how it is run today, honestly, rather
than a structure it has not grown into yet.

## Today

The project is maintained by [@AlzyWelzy](https://github.com/AlzyWelzy), who has
final say on direction, merges and releases.

That is not meant to be permanent. As contributors establish themselves, commit
access follows.

## Decisions

Most decisions happen in the open in issues and pull requests. Anyone may open
either; anyone may comment.

- **Ordinary changes** — bug fixes, content rendering, styling: a maintainer
  review and merge.
- **Substantial changes** — new dependencies, architectural shifts, anything
  affecting how the site ingests or licenses content: open an issue first and
  let it be discussed before writing the code. A rejected PR after a week of
  work is a bad outcome for everyone.
- **Licensing and attribution** are not up for negotiation. The site's
  obligations to the upstream wiki under CC BY-SA 4.0 are load-bearing.

## Becoming a maintainer

There is no application. Contribute consistently, review others' work
thoughtfully, and you will be invited. What is looked for:

- A track record of merged changes.
- Judgement about what belongs in the project and what does not.
- Care with the upstream wiki, whose infrastructure we depend on.

Maintainers can merge PRs, triage issues, and are expected to uphold the
[Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Disagreements

Discuss it in the issue. If consensus does not emerge, the maintainer decides
and explains why. If you think that is wrong, the AGPL guarantees your right to
fork — that is a feature of free software, not a failure of it.

## If the project is abandoned

If no maintainer responds for six months, contributors should consider the
project unmaintained and are encouraged to fork it. The licence exists precisely
so that this is possible without anyone's permission.
