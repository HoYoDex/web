# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want HoYoDex to be a place where anyone who loves these games can contribute,
regardless of experience, background, identity, or which gacha character they
rolled for.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings.
- Accept that people have different levels of experience, and help rather than
  gatekeep.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Datamined content shared in violation of embargoes, or leaks presented as
  confirmed fact.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **tech@hoyodex.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, and PRs that violate this
policy, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or
severe violations.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, PRs, discussions —
and when representing the project publicly.
